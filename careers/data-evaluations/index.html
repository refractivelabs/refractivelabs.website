Open role

Member of Technical Staff

Focus: Data & Evaluations
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll build the datasets and evaluations that drive progress in our ML models and AI agents. This means deciding which capabilities to measure, what a successful engineering result looks like, and which data will help our systems improve.

The work spans experimental design, training data, and evaluation infrastructure. You’ll work closely with ML researchers and photonics engineers to turn real design problems into reproducible tasks, useful learning signals, and reliable measures of progress.

What you’ll work on

  • Build datasets for models and agents. Bring together simulation results, engineering workflows, agent trajectories, and physical measurements, with the context needed to reproduce and interpret each example.
  • Design evaluations for engineering tasks. Build benchmarks that test whether agents can set up simulations, optimize devices, generate layouts, and verify their work against physical and manufacturing constraints.
  • Develop reference results and automated scoring. Create evaluators that measure correctness, physical performance, reliability, and computational cost.
  • Investigate failures and guide improvements. Analyze where models and agents struggle, design experiments to understand why, and turn those findings into targeted data and training priorities.
  • Improve how we generate and select data. Develop synthetic tasks, controlled variations, and methods for choosing informative training examples and simulations.
  • Make experiments trustworthy. Build reproducible evaluation pipelines, maintain data quality and provenance, and test generalization across devices, geometries, and operating conditions.

What we’re looking for

  • Strong Python programming and software engineering skills.
  • Practical experience with machine learning, including building datasets, evaluating models, or conducting empirical ML research.
  • Good experimental judgment and statistical reasoning: you can turn an ambiguous question into a measurable experiment and recognize when a metric is misleading.
  • Experience working with complex data and reasoning about data quality, sampling, train/test separation, and generalization.
  • The ability to take ownership of an open-ended problem and carry it through to working code and clear conclusions.

Experience with agent evaluations, synthetic data, active learning, reinforcement learning environments, or scientific computing would be especially relevant.

We welcome engineers who enjoy research and researchers who enjoy building systems. Prior photonics experience is helpful, but not required.

How to apply

Send a note and whatever best shows your work, whether that is a paper, a repository, a model, or a measurement. Mention the Data & Evaluations role so we know which one you mean.
