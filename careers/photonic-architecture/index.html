Open role

Member of Technical Staff

Focus: Photonic Architecture
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll define the architecture of the photonic chips we build: which circuit solves the problem, which components it needs, and what each of them has to achieve. You’ll carry those decisions from system requirements through block specifications, tapeout, and validation on measured silicon.

You’ll work across component design, layout, test, and machine learning. Part of the role is deciding what we build; part is helping our models and agents reason about circuits at the level an architect does.

What you’ll work on

  • Define chip architectures. Translate application and system requirements into circuit architectures, block-level specifications, and optical power and loss budgets.
  • Make the tradeoffs. Compare options for modulation, detection, wavelength multiplexing, and optical coupling by performance, power, footprint, yield, and packaging, and decide which to build.
  • Model circuits at the system level. Build circuit- and link-level simulations that capture noise, bandwidth, crosstalk, thermal effects, and process variation.
  • Lead designs through tapeout. Set milestones, coordinate component design, layout, and verification, and resolve the issues that stand between a design and fabrication.
  • Design for electronics and packaging. Account for the driver, receiver, control, and packaging constraints that determine whether a photonic circuit works outside the lab.
  • Close the loop with measurement. Define validation plans, compare measured performance against the budget, and feed what you learn into the next design.
  • Shape how AI designs circuits. Work with our machine learning team to express architecture-level problems as design spaces, constraints, and objectives that models and agents can explore.

What we’re looking for

  • A deep understanding of integrated photonic devices and circuits, including waveguides, couplers, ring and Mach-Zehnder modulators, photodetectors, and wavelength multiplexers, and of how they behave together in a system.
  • Experience architecting photonic integrated circuits and taking them from specification through tapeout and silicon validation.
  • Strong system-level reasoning about link budgets, noise, bandwidth, and thermal and process variation.
  • Fluency with photonic circuit simulation, and the programming skills to build your own models in Python.
  • Technical leadership: you can set scope, make architecture decisions, and bring a team of specialists to agreement.

Experience with high-speed optical transceivers, wavelength-division multiplexed architectures, co-packaged optics, programmable photonic meshes, or electronic-photonic co-design would be especially relevant.

We welcome architects who still enjoy working through the details of a device or a layout. Prior machine learning experience is helpful, but not required.

Compensation and benefits

The expected base salary range for this role is $200,000 to $370,000 per year. Where an offer falls within that range depends on experience and skills.

We also offer health, dental, and vision insurance and a 401(k) plan.

How to apply

Send a note and whatever best shows your work, whether that is a paper, a chip you architected, a model, or a measurement. Mention the Photonic Architecture role so we know which one you mean.
