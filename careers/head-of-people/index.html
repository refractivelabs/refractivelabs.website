Head of People

Focus: Recruiting & HR
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll lead recruiting and HR at Refractive Labs. That means owning how we find, hire, and support people across every role in the company, from machine learning researchers to photonic designers and test engineers.

This is a hands-on leadership role. You’ll set the direction for how we hire and how we operate as an employer, and you’ll also do the work yourself: sourcing candidates, running searches, and handling the day-to-day HR that a growing team depends on. You’ll work directly with the founders and with every hiring manager.

What you’ll work on

  • Lead recruiting across the company. Own hiring for all of our roles, technical and otherwise. Build the pipeline, run searches from first contact through offer, and make sure each one closes.
  • Find people who are hard to find. Source candidates in machine learning and integrated photonics, where the best people are rarely looking, and learn the fields well enough to recognize strong work.
  • Build a hiring process that is rigorous and respectful. Work with hiring managers to define roles, structure interviews, and reach clear decisions, while giving every candidate a prompt and considerate experience.
  • Run HR. Own onboarding, compensation and benefits, employment policies, and compliance, and work with outside counsel and vendors where needed.
  • Support people once they are here. Be the person employees and managers come to with questions and problems, and help managers give feedback, set expectations, and develop their teams.
  • Shape how we work as we grow. Help the founders define and sustain the culture and practices of the company, and plan headcount and organization as the team expands.
  • Represent Refractive Labs. Shape how we present ourselves to candidates, from job descriptions and this page to universities, conferences, and the research community.

What we’re looking for

  • Experience leading recruiting, HR, or both, ideally at an early-stage technology company.
  • A track record of hiring strong technical people, such as engineers and researchers, in a competitive market.
  • Working knowledge of HR fundamentals, including compensation, benefits, employment law and compliance in California, and employee relations.
  • Good judgment and discretion with sensitive and confidential matters.
  • Clear, direct communication with candidates, employees, and founders.
  • The willingness to build from scratch and do the hands-on work yourself.

Experience recruiting in machine learning, semiconductors, or photonics, hiring PhD-level researchers, supporting immigration and visa processes, or building a people function at a company that grew quickly would be especially relevant.

You do not need a technical background, but you should be curious about what we build and willing to learn enough about it to speak credibly with the people we want to hire.
