Open role

Member of Technical Staff

Focus: AI Agents & ML Systems
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll develop machine learning models and AI agents that help us design better photonic chips. This includes models that learn from simulation and experimental data to predict device behavior, agents that use those predictions to explore and optimize designs, and the execution systems that make these workflows reliable.

You’ll work across research and implementation, connecting model development to real engineering problems. Your initial focus will reflect your strengths in scientific machine learning, agent systems, or both.

What you’ll work on

  • Build predictive models of photonic devices. Train models to predict physical behavior and device performance from geometry, materials, and operating conditions.
  • Accelerate design exploration and optimization. Integrate learned models into design workflows so that agents can evaluate ideas, compare alternatives, and identify promising designs for further simulation and fabrication.
  • Establish where model predictions can be trusted. Evaluate accuracy, generalization, and uncertainty against physics simulations and measurements, and determine when additional simulation or data is needed.
  • Build agents for engineering workflows. Develop systems that plan experiments, write and execute code, use simulation and layout tools, interpret results, and refine designs.
  • Develop the execution harness. Build tool interfaces, context and state management, experiment tracking, and recovery mechanisms that support reliable workflows across many steps.
  • Improve models and agents through experimentation. Investigate model architectures, planning, tool use, fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning, using engineering tasks and evaluation results to guide decisions.
  • Apply the work to real devices. Collaborate with photonics engineers and the data and evaluations team to improve design quality, reliability, and computational efficiency.

What we’re looking for

  • Strong Python programming and software engineering skills.
  • Practical experience building, evaluating, and debugging ML models or agent systems.
  • Depth in at least one relevant area: scientific machine learning, LLM agents and tool use, or model training and post-training.
  • Strong experimental judgment and the ability to reason about tradeoffs between accuracy, speed, reliability, and computational cost.
  • The ability to move between open-ended research questions and reliable, maintainable implementations.

Experience with scientific computing, numerical optimization, uncertainty estimation, reinforcement learning, or distributed execution would be especially relevant.

We welcome engineers who enjoy research and researchers who enjoy building systems. You should bring depth in one area and an interest in working across the others. Prior photonics experience is helpful, but not required.

Benefits

We offer health, dental, and vision insurance and a 401(k) plan.

How to apply

Send a note and whatever best shows your work, whether that is a paper, a repository, a model, or a measurement. Mention the AI Agents & ML Systems role so we know which one you mean.
