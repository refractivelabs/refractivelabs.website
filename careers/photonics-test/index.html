Open role

Member of Technical Staff

Focus: Photonics Test
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll build the measurement capability that tells us whether our chips work. This means defining test methods, building and automating setups, and characterizing the devices and circuits we tape out.

Measured data matters twice here. It shows whether a design met its targets, and it is what our compact models and machine learning models are validated against and trained on. You’ll work closely with photonic designers and with the data and evaluations team to make that data reliable.

What you’ll work on

  • Develop test methods and plans. Decide what to measure for each device and circuit, under which conditions, and to what accuracy.
  • Build and automate test setups. Assemble wafer- and chip-scale optical and electro-optic measurement setups, and write the instrument control, alignment, and automation software that runs them.
  • Characterize devices and circuits. Measure passive and active components and full circuits, including loss, spectral response, bandwidth, and tuning behavior.
  • Help design test structures. Work with designers so that the quantities we care about can be extracted from the structures we fabricate.
  • Turn measurements into reliable data. Establish calibration, repeatability, uncertainty, and provenance, so that results can validate models and serve as training data.
  • Analyze results and feed them back. Use statistical analysis across dies, wafers, and fabrication runs to identify process variation and failure modes, and bring those findings to the design team.
  • Improve throughput and quality. Reduce test cycle time, and work with foundry, packaging, and equipment vendors as our testing grows.

What we’re looking for

  • Hands-on experience testing integrated photonic devices at wafer or chip scale.
  • Experience building and automating optical test setups, including tunable lasers, detectors, optical spectrum analyzers, probe stations, and fiber alignment.
  • Strong Python skills for instrument control and data analysis.
  • Good statistical judgment, including experimental design and measurement uncertainty.
  • Enough understanding of photonic device behavior to recognize when a measurement is wrong.
  • Clear written and verbal communication.

Experience with high-speed electro-optic measurements, automated wafer-scale probing, optical packaging and fiber attach, test structure design, or reliability testing would be especially relevant.

We welcome test engineers who enjoy writing software and designers who enjoy the lab. Prior machine learning experience is helpful, but not required.

Benefits

We offer health, dental, and vision insurance and a 401(k) plan.

How to apply

Send a note and whatever best shows your work, whether that is a paper, a test setup you built, a repository, or a measurement. Mention the Photonics Test role so we know which one you mean.
