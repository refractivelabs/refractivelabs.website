Open role

Member of Technical Staff

Focus: Photonic Component Design & Layout
Location: Campbell, CA

About Refractive Labs

Refractive Labs designs and develops photonic chips, with AI at the core of our approach. We combine machine learning, physics simulation, and experimental data to develop and optimize photonic devices and circuits. Our work spans models, chip design, and validation, connecting advances in AI to hardware we can fabricate and measure. We’re an early-stage team backed by Khosla Ventures.

About the role

You’ll design and lay out the photonic components and circuits we tape out. The work runs from device simulation through parameterized layout, compact models, and verification, and continues until the measured silicon agrees with the design.

You’ll work closely with our machine learning team. The components, design flows, and reference results you build are what our models and agents learn from and work with, so your designs matter twice: as hardware we fabricate, and as the foundation for automating the design work.

What you’ll work on

  • Design photonic components. Develop passive and active devices such as waveguides, couplers, filters, modulators, and photodetectors, using electromagnetic simulation to balance performance, footprint, and fabrication tolerance.
  • Lay out devices and circuits in code. Build parameterized cells and block- and chip-level layouts with script-based tools, keeping layout, netlist, and model consistent with one another.
  • Build compact models. Create behavioral models for circuit simulation and fit them to simulation results and measurements, so that circuit-level predictions match what we fabricate.
  • Verify designs and take them to tapeout. Run design rule checks, connectivity checks, and layout-driven simulation against foundry requirements, and resolve what they find before the design ships.
  • Design test structures. Work with our test engineers to define structures and measurements that extract device figures of merit and show where models and silicon disagree.
  • Make design flows that models and agents can use. Build layout, simulation, and verification flows that are scripted and reproducible, so they can be run automatically and at scale.
  • Contribute to our component library. Develop and maintain the design kit we build on, across foundry processes, with regression tests that keep existing designs working as it changes.

What we’re looking for

  • A strong grasp of integrated photonics fundamentals and silicon photonic device physics.
  • Experience designing photonic devices with electromagnetic simulation, such as FDTD, mode solvers, or eigenmode expansion, and a good sense of the tradeoffs involved.
  • Experience with script-based photonic layout in Python, using GDSFactory, gdstk, KLayout, or similar tools.
  • Tapeout experience: you have taken designs through foundry design rules to fabrication, and ideally measured the result.
  • Strong Python programming skills.
  • The ability to work independently on both tightly specified and loosely defined problems.

Experience with compact modeling and photonic circuit simulators, foundry or custom design kits, Cadence Virtuoso, inverse design, or automated testing of design libraries would be especially relevant.

We welcome device designers who enjoy writing software and software-minded engineers with real depth in photonics. Prior machine learning experience is helpful, but not required.
