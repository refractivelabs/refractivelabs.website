The bandwidth wall

Compute is outrunning the wires.

Interconnect bandwidth grows about 1.4× every two years. Compute grows about 3×. Every generation of AI hardware widens that gap, and silicon photonics is the only technology on a path to close it.

But photonic design is bottlenecked on people. Demand for photonics PhDs outstrips supply roughly ten to one, design cycles run 18 months or more, and over 60% of a project goes to design and verification rather than building anything new.

The platform

Three pillars, one workflow.

01

Proprietary data

A synthetic data factory paired with in-house characterization runs. We tape out our own test chips for under $100k so our models learn from measured silicon, not just simulation.

02

Unified workflow

Simulation, physics models, layout, and DRC/LVS in a single environment. Design, verify, and sign off without stitching together a dozen tools.

03

Adaptive intelligence

Design models tuned per project and per process. The platform gets better on your components, your foundry, and your constraints the more you use it.
